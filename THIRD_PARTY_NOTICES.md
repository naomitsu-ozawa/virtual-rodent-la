## Digimouse-derived anatomy preview

The public demo contains a strongly decimated proof-of-concept anatomy model derived from the Digimouse FEM Mesh Version 1L supplied for this project.

The demo currently exposes six layers: skin, skeleton, heart, liver, kidneys, and lungs.

The model is used only as an early interaction and rendering test. It is not presented as a complete dissection-training anatomy model.

Digimouse project: USC Biomedical Imaging Group.
