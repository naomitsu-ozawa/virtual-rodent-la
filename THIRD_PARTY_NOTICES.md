# Third-party notices

## Digimouse FEM Mesh Version 1L

The public demo uses the `face` surface-triangle data from Digimouse FEM Mesh Version 1L supplied for this project. The source archive states that the FEM mesh data and generation script are released in the public domain.

All 21 tissue surface IDs present in the supplied mesh are retained in the web model. Exact duplicate triangle records within an individual tissue surface are removed during conversion; no geometric decimation or tissue omission is applied.

Tissue IDs in the source archive:

1. skin
2. skeleton
3. eye
4. medulla
5. cerebellum
6. olfactory bulbs
7. external cerebrum
8. striatum
9. heart
10. rest of the brain
11. masseter muscles
12. lachrymal glands
13. bladder
14. testis
15. stomach
16. spleen
17. pancreas
18. liver
19. kidneys
20. adrenal glands
21. lungs

Related citation from the supplied archive: B. Dogdas, D. Stout, A. Chatziioannou, and R. M. Leahy, "Digimouse: A 3D Whole Body Mouse Atlas from CT and Cryosection Data." Phys Med Biol. 52(3):577-87, 2007.
